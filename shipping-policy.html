Shipping Policy

How we pack, ship and deliver your order

Last updated: August 2026

Delivery Coverage

Dhaan Foods currently ships the Smart Start Health Mix across India. We are working on expanding our courier network to reach every pincode — if your area isn't serviceable yet, our support team will contact you after you place an order.

Delivery Charges

A flat delivery charge of ₹40 applies per order, shown at checkout before you pay. This amount is added to the product price to arrive at your final total.

Delivery Timelines

  • Orders are processed within 24–48 hours of confirmation.
  • Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days for most locations.
  • Remote or non-metro pincodes may take up to 7 business days.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, a tracking update will be shared on the mobile number and email provided at checkout, wherever available.

Delayed or Failed Deliveries

Delays can occasionally occur due to weather, courier disruptions, or incomplete address details. If your order hasn't arrived within the expected window, please reach out to us and we'll investigate right away.
